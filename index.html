About

I'm a Master of International Relations candidate at the Catholic University of Eichstätt-Ingolstadt. My focus: how states negotiate over artificial intelligence.

My work follows German AI enforcement as it develops: which authorities hold enforcement powers, what they do first, and how the implementation of EU rules takes shape on the ground.
