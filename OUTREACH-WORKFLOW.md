# OUTREACH WORKFLOW — "Ship It, Then Wake It"

Simple rule: **no artifact ships without its outreach pass.** No quotas, no gates — just one routine that always runs right after publishing anything (F-brief, I-memo, tracker section/row batch).

---

## The Trigger

The moment ANY of these goes live:
- F-brief published on Substack
- I-memo completed
- Tracker: new rows added, new section launched, or a material status change

→ run the 4-step pass below (30–45 min, same sitting if possible).

## The 4 Steps

### 1. WHO — find the 3–5 humans attached to this piece (~10 min)
For whatever the piece is *about*, identify named people:
- Authors of the sources cited (press officers, authors of PDFs/studies)
- Researchers at target orgs (DGAP, Aspen DE, interface, AlgorithmWatch, EDRi, SNV, BNetzA/ministries) who publicly wrote on this exact topic
- Officials quoted or responsible (only where public contact exists)

Sources: the piece's own source list, LinkedIn search "[topic] Deutschland", org staff pages, Bundestag/agenda records.

### 2. WHY — one pretext line per person (~5 min)
One sentence each: *"Your work on X connects to this because Y."*
Rule: react to THEIR work, never pitch yourself. The artifact does the pitching.
(If a person has no honest connection, drop them — relevance beats volume.)

### 3. REACH — send within 48h (~15 min)
- Email preferred (professional address), LinkedIn connect request with note otherwise
- 3–5 sentences max. Template:
  > Hallo/Hello [Name] — I'm [Rex], I track Germany's AI-governance implementation in EN/DE at [link]. Your [piece/role] on [X] was directly relevant, so I thought you'd want to see [artifact]. If useful, happy to share the underlying row data. — Rex
- Log every send in `outreach-log.csv` (below) — no exceptions.

### 4. KEEP — follow-up & warm list (~5 min)
- No reply after ~10 days → ONE short follow-up ("bumping this once"), then stop.
- Any reply → mark WARM in log. Warm contacts get the next piece too, before anyone else.
- Never add the same person twice for consecutive pieces (rotate; keep a `do-not-spam` feel via the log's last-contacted column).

## The Log

`outreach-log.csv` columns:
`date, name, org, role, trigger_artifact, channel, status(sent/followup/warm/noreply), notes`

Sunday review (existing habit): glance at the log only. If the last 2 weeks show zero sends despite artifacts shipping → the trigger is broken; fix the habit, not the person.
